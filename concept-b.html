01 · Taste your way in

Indonesian food is made for a table.

Choose something grilled, something gently stewed, something tangy and—if you want it—something hot. Our staff will happily guide you through tastes, textures and amounts.

Start with sate.
Then add a curry, vegetables and rice for the table.

Heat is your choice.

Ask for sambal if you want more. All meat is halal; vegetarian dishes are available. Please tell us about allergies.
