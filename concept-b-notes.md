# Concept B — Indonesian Table

## 1. Design rationale

This direction makes sharing, abundance and Indonesian hospitality the primary brand idea. The visitor enters through food rather than history: large real photography, rounded serving shapes and a central rijsttafel experience make an unfamiliar cuisine feel generous and easy to approach.

## 2. Typography direction

- **Fraunces:** soft, expressive display serif for generous headlines and large numbers.
- **Figtree:** clear humanist sans for navigation, menu explanations and practical information.
- The contrast feels sociable and contemporary rather than polished or precious.

## 3. Colour system

- Coconut cream `#fbf4e4`
- Sambal red `#ba3e27`
- Turmeric yellow `#f0b63b`
- Deep leaf green `#294737`
- Toasted brown `#6a3d2a`
- A controlled pink-tan `#efd1bd` for the social-proof field

The colours come from food and tableware, not resort imagery.

## 4. Photography direction

Real Bali Bali dishes receive the greatest visual weight. Crops feel like plates arriving at a shared table: close, colourful and generous. Images are combined as simple table arrangements, never stock hospitality scenes or fabricated diners.

## 5. Graphic motifs

Circles echo serving plates; rounded labels behave like dish markers; offset borders recall stacked tableware. The motif is social and food-led rather than decorative batik or generic palm foliage.

## 6. Hero composition

A saturated green copy field meets a large food photograph. The invitation “Come hungry. Share everything.” leads, while a circular dietary note and red trust strip quickly communicate halal meat, family ownership, pre-theatre dining and the Shaftesbury Avenue location.

## 7. Section composition

Food is presented through one dominant image, one supporting image and a tactile flavour note. Rijsttafel is the visual centre: a circular plate image, large sharing facts and small flavour labels. Later sections retain generous shapes and colour blocks rather than switching to generic cards.

## 8. CTA treatment

Rounded, tactile and high-contrast. Sambal red marks booking actions; cream buttons support menus and directions. A lightweight text CTA represents group dining.

## 9. Mobile behaviour

The design becomes a vertical food story with edge-to-edge crops, compact dietary cues and a reduced plate composition. Colour fields stay bold but short. A fixed Book/Menu/Call bar prioritises conversion, and the navigation opens as a simple cream tray.
