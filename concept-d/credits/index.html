Candi Bentar

Candi Bentar by nusaheritage.id, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Adaptations for this website: Removed detached scan fragments and peripheral vegetation/ground; normalised the mesh around its passage; simplified geometry and resized/recompressed textures.

Frangipani / Jepun

Frangipani Flower by elkid, released under CC0 1.0.

Adaptations for this website: Kept the five original petals, UVs and photographed petal texture; evaluated subdivision and thickness; removed source cameras/lights; centred and scaled the mesh, converted its legacy material to PBR, and resized/compressed the texture for three web detail levels.

Balinese Penjor

Balinese Penjor by alterego.visual, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Adaptations for this website: Baked original node transforms, centred and scaled the complete bamboo bow and ornaments, and simplified geometry into three web detail levels. Original material colours and ornament arrangement retained.

Dwarapala guardian

Patung Dwarapala Pintu Utara by nusaheritage.id, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Adaptations for this website: Baked source node transforms; removed one detached 83-triangle scan fragment below/behind the statue; retained the complete statue and pedestal; centred/scaled the mesh, smoothed duplicate scan normals while retaining UV seams, simplified geometry and resized/recompressed the original photographed textures into three web detail levels. No opening was cut into the statue.
