# Concept A — London Institution

## 1. Design rationale

Bali Bali is treated as a quietly established independent London restaurant rather than a themed destination. The page borrows the authority and intimacy of a well-edited cultural journal: strong type, ruled structure, warm paper and present-day food photography. Heritage is communicated through the year, address and editorial voice—not fake archival imagery.

## 2. Typography direction

- **Newsreader:** literary, characterful serif for the logo, headlines, years and quotations.
- **DM Sans:** restrained contemporary grotesk for navigation, facts, labels and CTAs.
- Large, closely set headlines create authority; compact uppercase metadata gives the page an issue-like editorial rhythm.

## 3. Colour system

- Warm paper `#f2eadc` and lifted paper `#fbf7ef`
- Oxblood `#742f32` for heritage emphasis and primary actions
- Dense ink `#20211d` for text and footer
- Dark botanical green `#31443a` for depth without tropical imagery
- Mustard `#c5902d` as a limited food-derived accent

## 4. Photography direction

Current Bali Bali food photographs are framed as honest editorial documents. Images use inset borders, captions and asymmetric pairings rather than glossy full-bleed fine-dining treatment. No fake people or historical photography is used.

## 5. Graphic motifs

Hairline rules, edition labels, circular year stamps, marginal captions and measured numbering evoke independent publishing and restaurant menus. The references remain typographic and material, avoiding literal Indonesian stereotypes.

## 6. Hero composition

An asymmetric 58/42 split pairs a very large editorial headline with a framed food image. An “Est. 1986” issue line, year stamp and four trust signals establish heritage, West End location, halal meat and pre-theatre relevance before the visitor scrolls.

## 7. Section composition

The page alternates editorial columns, ruled lists and full-width colour fields. Signature food reads like a menu feature; rijsttafel becomes a deep oxblood spread with a large “8”; family heritage uses a monumental date rather than fabricated memorabilia.

## 8. CTA treatment

Rectangular, compact and typographic. Oxblood indicates the primary booking action; outlined paper buttons handle menu and directions. Group dining remains a small underlined phone enquiry.

## 9. Mobile behaviour

Asymmetry collapses into a deliberate reading order. The date and trust signals remain near the top, editorial image pairings simplify, and a fixed Book/Menu/Call bar keeps practical actions available. Type scales with `clamp()` and lists become single-column without clipping.
